# Gaurab Nandi Das

**Butterfly ecologist · Biodiversity researcher**

I am a butterfly ecologist and biodiversity researcher interested in understanding how butterfly diversity, communities and phenotypes vary across space and through time.

My research combines field ecology, taxonomy, evolutionary approaches, biodiversity informatics and quantitative analysis to investigate patterns of biodiversity and environmental change.

## Research interests

My current interests include:

- Butterfly ecology and community ecology
- Biodiversity and biogeography
- Taxonomy and evolutionary relationships
- Natural history collections
- Biodiversity informatics
- Phenotypic variation and change
- Environmental and climatic change
- Conservation of butterfly diversity

## Current research

I am particularly interested in connecting historical and contemporary biodiversity information.

My work includes the use of natural history museum collections, biodiversity databases, field observations and environmental data to investigate long-term patterns in butterfly diversity and phenotypic variation.

A major component of this work is **TRACE**, an interdisciplinary research project focused on reconstructing long-term phenotypic responses of South Asian butterflies to global change.

## Research approach

I work across several complementary areas:

**Field ecology**  
Documenting butterfly diversity, abundance and habitat associations.

**Natural history collections**  
Using historical specimens as records of past biodiversity and phenotype.

**Biodiversity informatics**  
Integrating museum and biodiversity datasets to connect historical and contemporary observations.

**Evolutionary analysis**  
Using phylogenetic information to investigate evolutionary patterns in butterfly diversity.

**Quantitative ecology**  
Applying statistical and spatial approaches to understand biodiversity patterns and environmental relationships.

## Research location

I am currently based in **Czechia** and work on biodiversity research with a strong focus on South Asian butterflies.
