# Fieldwork

Much of my research begins in the field, documenting butterfly diversity and the habitats in which species occur.

My fieldwork focuses primarily on butterflies of the Indian subcontinent, combining standardized biodiversity surveys with ecological observations and environmental measurements.

## Manas National Park

**Assam, India**

My field research in Manas National Park examines butterfly diversity across contrasting habitats and environmental gradients.

The work includes standardized surveys of butterfly communities and assessment of habitat characteristics.

### Research questions

- How does butterfly richness vary among habitats?
- How do butterfly communities respond to environmental gradients?
- Which habitat characteristics are associated with butterfly diversity?
- How do survey approaches influence estimates of butterfly richness and composition?

### Field methods

Field surveys combine standardized observation periods with habitat and environmental measurements.

The resulting data are used to examine:

**Species richness** · **Abundance** · **Community composition** · **Habitat associations** · **Environmental gradients**
