# Projects

My research projects bring together field ecology, biodiversity informatics, evolutionary approaches and natural history collections to understand butterfly diversity and environmental change.

## Featured projects

### TRACE

**Tracing long-term phenotypic responses of South Asian butterflies to global change**

TRACE investigates how butterfly phenotypes have changed through time by integrating historical natural history collections with contemporary biodiversity and environmental data.

The project combines:

- Natural history museum collections
- Historical and contemporary biodiversity records
- Geometric morphometrics
- Biodiversity informatics
- Environmental and climatic data
- Spatial analysis
- Quantitative modelling

### Butterfly biodiversity in Manas National Park

**Butterfly communities, habitat associations and environmental gradients**

This project investigates butterfly diversity across habitats in Manas National Park, Assam, India.

The research combines standardized field surveys with environmental and habitat information to understand patterns of:

- Species richness
- Abundance
- Community composition
- Habitat associations
- Environmental gradients

**→ Explore the Manas project**

### Indian Butterfly Biogeography

**Distribution, endemism and evolutionary distinctiveness of Indian butterflies**

This project examines large-scale patterns of butterfly distributions across India, including species range size, endemism and evolutionary distinctiveness.

The work integrates:

- Species distribution data
- Phylogenetic information
- Evolutionary distinctiveness
- Endemism
- Biogeographic patterns
- Biodiversity databases
