## Research themes

**Butterfly ecology**  
Species diversity, abundance, communities and habitat associations.

**Biodiversity & biogeography**  
Species distributions, endemism and large-scale biodiversity patterns.

**Evolution & phylogenetics**  
Evolutionary relationships and evolutionary distinctiveness of butterflies.

**Natural history collections**  
Historical specimens and their potential for understanding long-term biological change.

**Environmental change**  
Relationships between biodiversity, phenotypes and changing environmental conditions.
