## Research projects

### TRACE

**Tracing long-term phenotypic responses of South Asian butterflies to global change**

An interdisciplinary research project integrating natural history collections, biodiversity informatics, computational approaches, geometric morphometrics and environmental modelling.

### Butterfly biodiversity in Manas National Park

Research on butterfly diversity, abundance, community composition and habitat associations across habitats and environmental gradients.

### Indian Butterfly Biogeography

Research examining butterfly distributions, endemism, evolutionary distinctiveness and large-scale biogeographic patterns across India.
